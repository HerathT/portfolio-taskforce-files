ENV-03

Edge cron jobs installed and firing

Critical Functional

The entire sync pipeline depends on five edge-side cron jobs running on schedule; a missing or misconfigured cron silently breaks offline trading without any visible error on the till.

Steps to reproduce

  1. On the edge box, list the crontab and confirm all five jobs are present: hydrate, push_outbox, expire_holds, poll_commands, heartbeat.
  2. Confirm each crontab entry cds into the application root before running (a missing cd causes .env to silently fail to load).
  3. Tail the cron log for at least 5 minutes and confirm each job fires on its expected cadence (every 1 minute, except expire_holds at 2 minutes) with no errors.

Expected result

All five jobs are installed, correctly rooted, and firing cleanly on schedule with zero errors in the log.

ENV-05

TLS trust chain and insecure-TLS flag absent

Critical Security
